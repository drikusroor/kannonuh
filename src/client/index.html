How to lay siege

  • Aim by dragging from your gun, or with the elevation and powder dials. Arrow keys nudge; hold shift for fine steps.
  • Wind pushes light shot around. Heavy bombs care less about it.
  • Every gun may fire once per volley, up to three shots a turn.
  • Credits come from damage and kills. Spend them on shells, guns, upgrades and walls at the Quartermaster.
  • Powder barrels chain-detonate. Look for them near the enemy keep.
  • Win by breaking through the keep and killing the enemy king. Do it fast and accurately for a higher score.
  • Camera: scroll to zoom, drag with right mouse (or two fingers) to pan, 🔭 refits the field.
